01

What every Nunik Co. website includes

Every build is designed for your business and made to win the enquiry, not to look like a template.

  • Custom, mobile-first design. Most of your visitors are on a phone, so that is where we start.
  • A fast build. Static pages with no WordPress plugins to update, patch or slow the site down.
  • One clear call to action. Book a time, ask for a quote or message on WhatsApp, whichever suits how your customers buy.
  • Proof where it counts. Reviews, photos of real work, licences and prices placed where people decide.

Add the SEO, AEO and GEO foundation and we also set up the technical side of being found: structured data (schema) for your business, services and area, page titles and descriptions for every page, Google Search Console, redirects from an old site, an llms.txt file, and access for the crawlers that ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini use.

02

What happens after someone clicks

A website only earns its keep if enquiries get answered. We can connect your forms to an instant reply, a same-minute alert to your phone and a follow-up email for people who start an enquiry and do not finish it, so a busy day on the tools does not cost you the job. That is our automation work, and it is often worth more than the website itself.

Local SEO in Canberra, built for Google and AI search

Local customers find businesses in three places: the Google map results, a handful of "service plus suburb" searches, and increasingly in answers from AI assistants. We build for all three: clear answers to the questions customers ask, consistent business details, schema markup and pages every crawler can read. After launch, our small business SEO retainer is $300 or $550 a month with a three month minimum. If the terms are new, start with what AEO and GEO mean.

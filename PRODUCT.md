## Register

**Brand.** This is a single-page marketing site for an AI agency. The visitor's impression IS the deliverable. No product UI, no in-app surfaces — every page exists to communicate, build trust, and route owners to `hello@nunik.co`.

## Product purpose

Nunik Co. sells a "front door → engine room" model:

- **Front door** (project work, one-off fees): website builds, brand identity systems. Most clients meet Nunik Co. through these.
- **Engine room** (recurring retainer): n8n automation, GoHighLevel CRM, booking/follow-up plumbing, website care.

The strategic move: land with the front door, expand into the engine room. Every web build should be scoped with the automation retainer already in mind.

## Users (ICP)

Owner-led businesses that care how they come across, have real money moving, and are tired of looking and running like everyone else.

| Tier | Who | Where the money is |
|---|---|---|
| **ICP 1 (priority)** | Established local-service SMBs. Trades, allied health, clinics, mobile services. Teams of 3-20, revenue $300k-$3M. Prototype: Richill Pest Control. | Recurring retainer (automation, CRM, booking, follow-up). The line that compounds. |
| **ICP 2** | Hospitality and experience venues. Canberra-centric, brand-led, owner-operated. Prototype: Dada Restaurant, Olive at Hawker. | Project work (brand + web). Low recurring, high portfolio value. |
| **ICP 3 (selective)** | Founder and product brands. Showcase work. Prototype: Velara, Georgyracer, FindItPal, TAPR. | Project work, episodic. Wins the next three clients by portfolio strength. |

The buyer is almost always the owner — fast decisions, design-aware on the hospitality and founder side, price-sensitive but trust-driven on the trades side.

**Anti-ICP**: pure SEO/content/paid-media seekers (out of scope), race-to-the-bottom buyers, order-takers who want a yes-man, pre-revenue with no budget.

## Brand stance

South Asian heritage digital collective based in Canberra, Australia, delivering remotely worldwide. The work is framed by three Sanskrit principles, used as operating philosophy, not decoration:

- **Yukti** (युक्ति) — Strategic execution. AI automation and workflow engineering.
- **Rasa** (रस) — Aesthetic emotion. Brand identity and visual systems.
- **Artha** (अर्थ) — Purpose and prosperity. High-performance web and strategic consulting.

The brand promise: *Intelligent systems. Flawless execution. Built for the physical world.*

The reach is borderless. Canberra and Australia are the home base and the local-SEO anchor, never a limit on who the brand serves. Trust and execution lead the message; location is an SEO surface.

## Tone

- Authoritative, precise, premium, restrained. Confident without hype.
- Australian English at all times (`organise`, `optimise`, `colour`, `behaviour`, `centre`, `defence`).
- **Never em dashes or en dashes.** Use commas, colons, parentheses, semicolons, full stops.
- Direct subject-verb sentence openers. No "Furthermore", "Moreover", "In essence", "Additionally", "Therefore".
- Banned tics: `leverage`, `delve`, `tapestry`, `transformative`, `holistic`, `synergy`, `facilitate`, `utilise`, `seamless` (overuse), `elevate`, `robust` (unless statistical), "in today's fast-paced world", "it is worth noting", "could potentially".
- Lead every section with the answer or the value, not a warm-up sentence.
- No paragraph over three sentences. Vary sentence length.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero metric → 3-card feature grid → testimonial wall → CTA. Not this.
- Stripe-influenced minimal restraint copied without intent. Restraint without a POV reads as mediocre.
- Race-to-the-bottom Fiverr / Wix budget tier. Visual cliches that signal cheapness (random emoji icons above feature blocks, "Trusted by" logo strips with no real logos, low-contrast hero with a smiling stock photo).
- Editorial-magazine reflex (display serif + italic drop caps + ruled separators + monochrome restraint) used by default. We DO use display italic serif as part of the established identity, but the anti-reference is the *template* version of that lane.
- Heavy generic stock photography. The brand carries its voice through typography, the WebGL fluid background, the 3D Sudarshana Chakra, and the kinetic brand wordmark.

## Strategic design principles

- **Type does the heavy lifting.** Newsreader italic at display sizes for headlines; Inter for body; Material Symbols for icons. No additional fonts.
- **Two ambient WebGL layers carry the mood.** Fluid shader background (z-index -2) plus a subtle 3D Sudarshana Chakra (opacity 0.22, z-index -1) that traverses the viewport across sections via scroll waypoints. These are non-negotiable; they're the brand's voice.
- **Sanskrit principle blocks** (Yukti / Rasa / Artha) are the three Arsenal sections, each its own section, never collapsed into one grid.
- **Frosted-section alternation.** Sections alternate frosted-glass and transparent backgrounds so the WebGL layers periodically come forward and recede.
- **Brand chakra wordmark + apostrophe-dot.** The actual Adobe-traced wordmark SVG is the brand mark, including the swoosh flourish under "Co." It appears in the hero (kinetic per-path tilt) and the header (scroll-triggered fade-in).
- **Initiate Protocol** floating button appears on ALL screen sizes (mobile included).
- **All-device responsiveness.** iPhone, iPad, Android, Samsung, Mac, ZenBook Fold — every footprint.

## Strategic principles (operations)

Mirror the front-door → engine-room model in every page:

- The build is the hook. The automation retainer is the destination.
- Internal links flow authority from informational content (`/insights`, `/research`) down to money pages (`/services/automation`, `/services/brand`, `/services/web`, `/industries/trades`).
- Weight ICP 1 first in CTAs and content emphasis. ICP 1 is where the recurring revenue lives and where online competition is weakest.

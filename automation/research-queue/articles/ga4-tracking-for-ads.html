To tell if your ads are working, track enquiries, not clicks. Set up Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to record form submissions, phone calls and bookings as key events, connect those to Google Ads and Meta, tag every ad link with UTM parameters, and check cost per enquiry and cost per customer each week. Without this, you're judging ads on guesswork.

01

Clicks, impressions and likes are useful for diagnosing problems, but they don't tell you whether ads are making money.

02

Step-by-step setup

1. Install GA4 on your website

Create a GA4 property in Google Analytics and add the tag to every page of your site, either directly or through Google Tag Manager.

2. Track the actions that matter

Set up events for form submissions (ideally fired when the thank-you message appears), clicks on your phone number, clicks on WhatsApp or booking buttons, and completed bookings or purchases. Then mark them as key events in GA4, which is GA4's name for the actions you care most about.

3. Connect Google Ads

Link Google Ads to GA4 and import your key events as conversions, or set up conversion tracking with the Google tag. Add call tracking if phone calls are a big part of your business.

4. Set up the Meta pixel

Install the Meta pixel on your site and set up events for the same actions. The Conversions API, which sends events from your server, makes tracking more reliable as browsers block more cookies.

5. Tag every ad link with UTMs

Add UTM parameters to each ad's link so GA4 knows exactly where a visitor came from:

?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=summer_offer

Use the same naming every time, or your reports become a mess.

6. Ask every new customer how they found you

Tracking misses things: people who see an ad on their phone and call later from a laptop, or hear about you from a friend who saw it. A simple "how did you hear about us?" question fills the gaps.

7. Respect privacy choices

If your site shows a cookie banner, make sure your tracking follows the choices people make, and that your privacy policy explains what you collect.

03

Your weekly 10-minute check

  1. Spend this week, per platform
  2. Enquiries this week, per platform
  3. Cost per enquiry, compared with your target
  4. Any ad or keyword spending money with no enquiries, paused
  5. Enquiries from last week that became customers, logged

The way to get more Google reviews is to ask every customer, at the moment they're happiest, with a direct link that takes them straight to the review box. Make it a habit, not a campaign. Then reply to every review personally. Avoid incentives and avoid asking only the customers you expect to be happy, because both break Google's rules.

01

Why reviews matter so much

Reviews affect where you appear in Google Maps results, and they're usually the main thing a customer reads before deciding to call. A business with plenty of recent, genuine reviews looks busy, trusted and real. One with three reviews from 2022 makes people wonder if you're still open.

02

6 ways to get more reviews

1. Ask at the right moment

Ask when the customer has just experienced the result: the test is passed, the job is done, the meal is finished. Gratitude fades fast.

2. Send a direct link

Find your review link in your Google Business Profile and send it by text, WhatsApp or email. Every extra step loses people. A QR code on your counter, business card or invoice works for in-person businesses.

3. Make asking part of the routine

Add it to your end-of-job checklist, or automate it so a review request goes out when a job is marked complete. If it depends on you remembering, it won't happen consistently.

4. Keep the message short and personal

Something like: "Thanks for choosing us, [name]. If you've got a minute, a Google review really helps a small business like ours: [link]". Plain and human works best.

5. Reply to every review

Thank people by name and mention something specific. For negative reviews, stay calm, acknowledge the issue, and offer to sort it out directly. Future customers read your replies as closely as the reviews. At Rams Driving Academy, every review gets a personal reply in the owner's own voice, and the profile has passed 100 reviews.

6. Keep the pace natural

On a new profile especially, a sudden burst of reviews can look suspicious and get filtered. A steady flow over months is better than a spike in one week.

04

Frequently asked questions

Can I offer a discount in exchange for a Google review?

No. Google doesn't allow incentives for reviews, including discounts, freebies and prize draws. You can ask, but you can't reward.

Can I remove a bad Google review?

Only if it breaks Google's policies, for example if it's spam, offensive or not about a real experience. You can flag it. Otherwise, the best response is a calm, helpful reply.

Should I reply to every Google review?

Yes. It shows you're paying attention, and a thoughtful reply to a negative review can win over future customers.

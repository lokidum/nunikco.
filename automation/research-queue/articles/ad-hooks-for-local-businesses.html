An ad hook is the first line or first two seconds of your ad, the part that decides whether someone keeps watching or scrolls past. The hooks that work best for local businesses name a specific problem, mention the local area, show a real result, or say something surprising. A strong hook matters more than polished production.

01

The 10 hooks

#HookExample
1Call out the local area"Gungahlin parents, this one's for you."
2Name the exact problem"Hot water gone cold again?"
3The common mistake"Most learners fail their test on this one thing."
4The before and afterShow the clean gutter, the finished kitchen, the passed test first. Explain how after.
5The honest price"Here's exactly what a pest inspection costs in Canberra."
6The question they're already asking"How many driving lessons do you actually need?"
7The behind the scenes"Here's what happens to your order after you tap buy."
8The customer's wordsA real review read aloud, or a real customer on camera (with permission).
9The myth buster"No, you don't need to replace the whole roof."
10The face and the nameThe owner introducing themselves and the business, straight to camera.
02

Why these work

People stop for their own suburb, and it tells them you're nearby. Naming a problem they have today keeps them watching. The fear of an avoidable mistake is strong. Price transparency stands out because so few businesses do it. Answering a question people already search gives them a reason to stay for the answer. And a real person talking straight to camera builds trust fast, especially for small businesses.

03

How to use them

  • Write three hooks for the same ad, change nothing else, and test them against each other
  • Put the hook in the first one to two seconds, both spoken and as on-screen text
  • Add captions, because many people watch with the sound off
  • Keep the promise. If the hook mentions price, show the price
  • Avoid fake urgency and made-up claims. Australian Consumer Law applies to ads too

Once you have the hook, build the rest of the ad around it: proof, offer, then one clear action. More in how to run ads that actually work.

AEO (answer engine optimisation) means making your content easy for search engines and voice assistants to pull out as a direct answer. GEO (generative engine optimisation) means making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to understand, trust and recommend. Both build on good SEO. If your customers ask AI tools for recommendations, and more of them do every year, you need them.

02

How AI tools decide who to recommend

AI tools build answers from content they can read and trust. When someone asks "best driving school in Adelaide" or "who can fix my hot water system in Canberra", the tool looks for businesses with clear, consistent information across several sources. That includes your website, your Google profile, reviews and directory listings.

If your website is vague, blocks AI crawlers, or lists a different phone number from your Google profile, you're harder to recommend with confidence.

03

7 things to do first

  1. Answer real questions on your website. Put the direct answer in the first sentence, then the detail.
  2. Add an FAQ section to your home and service pages, using the questions customers actually ask.
  3. Add schema markup so search engines and AI tools can read your business details reliably.
  4. Let AI crawlers in. Check your robots.txt file doesn't block the bots used by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and others.
  5. Add an llms.txt file: a short plain-text summary of your business and key pages for AI tools.
  6. Keep your details identical everywhere: name, address, phone, hours.
  7. Publish specific facts: prices, service areas, years in business, licences, real results. Specific facts are easier to quote than vague claims.

04

How to check whether AI tools mention you

Once a month, ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude the questions your customers would ask, like "who's a good [your service] in [your suburb]?". Note whether you're mentioned and whether the details are correct. If they get something wrong, fix the source: usually your website or Google profile.

05

Frequently asked questions

Is SEO dead because of AI?

No. AEO and GEO build on the same foundations as SEO: useful content, a fast and technically sound site, and trust. Good SEO makes AEO and GEO much easier.

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT answers?

Not in the normal answers. Being mentioned comes from having clear, trustworthy information about your business across the web.

What is an llms.txt file?

A plain-text file at the root of your website that summarises your business and links to your most important pages, written for AI tools to read. It's a newer, emerging convention, quick to add.

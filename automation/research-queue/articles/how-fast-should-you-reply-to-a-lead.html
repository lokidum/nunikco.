Reply to a new lead as fast as you can: an automatic acknowledgement instantly, and a real, personal reply within the hour during business hours. Most people contact two or three businesses at once, and the first one that answers helpfully usually gets the job. Faster replies are often the cheapest way for a small business to win more customers, because you're converting people who already want to buy.

01

Why speed matters so much

When someone fills in your form or calls you, they have a need right now. Every hour you wait, three things can happen: they find someone else, the urgency fades, or they simply forget they asked. None of those show up in your reports. The lead just quietly goes cold.

03

How to reply faster when you're busy

  1. Set up instant acknowledgements. A friendly automatic reply confirming you got their message and when you'll respond.
  2. Get alerts where you'll see them. Send new leads to your phone as an SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram message, not just to an inbox you check at night.
  3. Use saved replies. Write your answers to the five most common questions once and reuse them.
  4. Cover missed calls. An instant text back, or an AI receptionist that takes the details for you.
  5. Block time to call back. A short window at lunch and at the end of the day for callbacks.
  6. Make one person responsible. If enquiries belong to everyone, they belong to no one.
04

Measure it

For one week, note when each enquiry came in and when you first replied personally. Most owners are surprised by the gaps. Once you see the number, it's easy to improve.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good lead response time?

An instant automatic acknowledgement, and a personal reply within the hour during business hours. Same day is the minimum.

Do automatic replies count as responding?

They help, because they stop people moving on while they wait. They don't replace a real, personal reply.

What should my first reply to a lead say?

Thank them by name, answer their question or confirm what they need, and suggest a clear next step, like a time to call or a booking link.

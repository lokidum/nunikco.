A client onboarding checklist lists everything you do between a customer saying yes and their first great experience with you: a welcome message, confirmation of what they've booked, what to prepare, reminders before the appointment, and a follow-up afterwards. Good onboarding cuts no-shows, reduces back-and-forth questions, and makes customers far more likely to come back and refer you.

01

Why onboarding matters for small businesses

The moment after someone says yes is when they're most likely to have second thoughts. They don't know exactly what happens next, they're not sure what to bring, and they're waiting. A clear, warm onboarding process answers all of that before they have to ask.

02

The checklist

Stage 1: Right after they say yes

  • Welcome message with your name, a photo and a direct contact number
  • Confirmation of what they've booked, the date, time and price
  • Invoice, deposit or payment link if needed
  • Any forms they need to fill in, kept as short as possible
  • Your cancellation or rescheduling policy, in plain words

Stage 2: Before the first appointment

  • A "what to expect" note: where, how long, what happens
  • What to bring or prepare
  • Directions or parking details, if they're coming to you
  • Reminder 24 hours before
  • Same-day reminder for early bookings

Stage 3: The first appointment

  • Arrive or be ready on time
  • Recap what you'll do today and what comes after
  • Ask if they have any questions

Stage 4: Straight after

  • A thank-you message the same day
  • Summary of what was done and any next steps
  • Book the next session or follow-up while they're happy

Stage 5: When the job is complete

  • Ask for a Google review with a direct link
  • Ask if they know anyone else who might need you
  • Ask permission to keep in touch, and note their consent
  • Add them to your customer list

Which steps to automate

StepAutomate?Why
Booking confirmationYesSame every time, needs to be instant
RemindersYesEasy to forget, and they cut no-shows

Review requestYesTrigger it when the job is marked complete

Handling questions or problemsNoThis is where the relationship is built

If you send marketing messages to past customers, Australia's Spam Act 2003 requires their consent, a clear identification of your business, and an easy way to unsubscribe.

04

Example: a driving school

For a driving school, onboarding might look like this: confirmation of the first lesson by WhatsApp, a note on where the instructor will pick the learner up and what to bring (their learner's permit), a reminder the evening before, a quick message after the lesson with what to practise, and a review request after the driving test.

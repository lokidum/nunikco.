Adelaide Confident Driving Academy (ACDA) is an Adelaide driving school that has been teaching learners since 2006. We rebranded the business from its old name, moved it to a new domain with every old page redirected, and built a fast website around WhatsApp booking. The site now gets around 300 clicks a month.

01

The problem

Gopi, the instructor behind the business, had a strong reputation and a perfect 5.0 Google rating, but an online presence that didn't match it. The old website even listed the wrong founding year, claiming 1990 when the business started in 2006.

He also offers two different tests, the CBT&A final drive and the VORT test in the instructor's vehicle, and wanted students to tell them apart at a glance.

02

What we did

Brand

We renamed the business Adelaide Confident Driving Academy. It says what the school does, where it is, and what students actually want to feel by the end.

The standout idea came from Gopi himself. He wanted each test colour-coded to match the real Australian plates students already know: the CBT&A final drive in the learner plate's yellow and black, and the VORT test in the P1 plate's red and white. We carried that system through the website, the business cards and the car magnets, so a student recognises it everywhere.

Print included a double-sided business card with a WhatsApp QR code, and vehicle door magnets in three sizes. At Gopi's request, we left the star rating off the printed pieces.

Website and growth

  • New domain with 301 redirects. We moved the business to adelaideconfidentdriving.com.au and redirected every old page to its new equivalent, so the history built under the old name carried across.
  • A hybrid layout. A single main page that tells the whole story, plus separate pages for each service, so each one can rank for its own searches.
  • WhatsApp as the main action. One clear button on every page. A nervous learner can ask a quick question without the pressure of a phone call.
  • Clear service areas. The site covers the southern suburbs, with Marion as the boundary, so the school stops getting enquiries from areas it doesn't serve.
  • Built for AI answers too. Structured data for the business and its services, FAQ sections answering real student questions, and settings that let AI crawlers read the site.
  • Fast and secure. A static build served through Cloudflare loads quickly on phones, which is how most learners and their parents browse.
03

Results

The site now gets around 300 clicks a month, with enquiries landing straight on Gopi's phone through WhatsApp.

Lessons

What other service businesses can take from this

  1. A rename doesn't have to reset your Google rankings. Map every old URL to a new one with 301 redirects before you switch.
  2. Use the channel your customers prefer. For ACDA, that's WhatsApp. For you, it might be calls or online booking.
  3. Let the client's own ideas lead. The plate colour system came from Gopi, and it's the most memorable part of the brand.
  4. Fix the small untruths. A wrong founding year looks minor, but trust is built on details.
04

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my business name without losing Google rankings?

Yes, if you plan it. Keep the old domain, redirect every old page to its matching new page with 301 redirects, update your Google Business Profile and listings, and tell Google about the move in Search Console.

Is WhatsApp a good booking channel for a service business?

For businesses where customers want to ask a quick question first, like driving schools, trades and beauty services, it often works better than a form. It feels personal and low-pressure.

What should a driving school website include?

Clear services and prices, the suburbs you cover, your reviews, instructor details and credentials, answers to common learner questions, and one easy way to book.

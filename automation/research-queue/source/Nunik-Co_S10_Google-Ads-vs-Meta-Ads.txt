      Google Ads vs Meta Ads for a Local
      Service Business
      How to choose between Google and Facebook or Instagram ads, with examples by
      industry and a simple way to split a small budget.
      By Lokesh Barani Kumar, founder of Nunik Co. · November 2026



Google Ads usually works better for local service businesses because it reaches people who are
already searching for what you offer. Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) usually work better for
businesses that need to create interest or show something visual, because they reach people
before they start searching. If you can only afford one, pick the one that matches how your
customers decide to buy.

The core difference: demand vs discovery
Google Ads captures demand. Someone types "emergency plumber Belconnen" and your ad
appears. They already want what you sell.
Meta ads create discovery. Someone is scrolling Instagram and sees your dessert truck's video.
They weren't looking for dessert, but now they want it.

Splitting a small budget
If you want to try both, don't split evenly from day one. Put most of your budget on the platform
that suits your business best, prove it works, then test the other with a smaller amount. Two half-
funded campaigns teach you less than one properly funded one.

When to use both together
They work well as a team. Meta ads build awareness, and Google Ads catches those people later
when they search. Retargeting on Meta can also remind people who visited your site but didn't
enquire.

      Adelaide Confident Driving Academy
      How we rebranded an Adelaide driving school, moved it to a new domain and built a
      WhatsApp booking site now getting around 300 clicks a month.
      By Lokesh Barani Kumar, founder of Nunik Co. · November 2026



Adelaide Confident Driving Academy (ACDA) is an Adelaide driving school that has been teaching
learners since 2006. We rebranded the business from its old name, moved it to a new domain with
every old page redirected, and built a fast website around WhatsApp booking. The site now gets
around 300 clicks a month.

The problem
Gopi, the instructor behind the business, had a strong reputation and a perfect 5.0 Google rating,
but an online presence that didn't match it. The old website even listed the wrong founding year,
claiming 1990 when the business started in 2006.
He also offers two different tests, the CBT&A final drive and the VORT test in the instructor's
vehicle, and wanted students to tell them apart at a glance.

What we did
Brand
We renamed the business Adelaide Confident Driving Academy. It says what the school does,
where it is, and what students actually want to feel by the end.
The standout idea came from Gopi himself. He wanted each test colour-coded to match the real
Australian plates students already know: the CBT&A final drive in the learner plate's yellow and
black, and the VORT test in the P1 plate's red and white. We carried that system through the
website, the business cards and the car magnets, so a student recognises it everywhere.

Website and growth
    New domain with 301 redirects. We moved the business to adelaideconfidentdriving.com.au
    and redirected every old page to its new equivalent, so the history built under the old name
    carried across.
    A hybrid layout. A single main page that tells the whole story, plus separate pages for each
    service, so each one can rank for its own searches.
    WhatsApp as the main action. One clear button on every page. A nervous learner can ask a
    quick question without the pressure of a phone call.
    Clear service areas. The site covers the southern suburbs, with Marion as the boundary, so the
    school stops getting enquiries from areas it doesn't serve.
    Built for AI answers too. Structured data for the business and its services, FAQ sections
    answering real student questions, and settings that let AI crawlers read the site.
    Fast and secure. A static build served through Cloudflare loads quickly on phones, which is how
    most learners and their parents browse.

Results
The site now gets around 300 clicks a month, with enquiries landing straight on Gopi's phone
through WhatsApp.

What other service businesses can take from this
1. A rename doesn't have to reset your Google rankings. Map every old URL to a new one with
   301 redirects before you switch.
2. Use the channel your customers prefer. For ACDA, that's WhatsApp. For you, it might be calls
   or online booking.
3. Let the client's own ideas lead. The plate colour system came from Gopi, and it's the most
   memorable part of the brand.
4. Fix the small untruths. A wrong founding year looks minor, but trust is built on details.

Frequently asked questions
Can I change my business name without losing Google rankings?
Yes, if you plan it. Keep the old domain, redirect every old page to its matching new page with 301
redirects, update your Google Business Profile and listings, and tell Google about the move in
Search Console.
Is WhatsApp a good booking channel for a service business?
For businesses where customers want to ask a quick question first, like driving schools, trades and
beauty services, it often works better than a form. It feels personal and low-pressure.

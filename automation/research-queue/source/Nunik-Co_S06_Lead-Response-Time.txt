      How Fast Should You Reply to a Lead?
      Why reply speed wins customers, the response times to aim for, and simple ways a
      busy owner can reply faster.
      By Lokesh Barani Kumar, founder of Nunik Co. · November 2026



Reply to a new lead as fast as you can: an automatic acknowledgement instantly, and a real,
personal reply within the hour during business hours. Most people contact two or three businesses
at once, and the first one that answers helpfully usually gets the job. Faster replies are often the
cheapest way for a small business to win more customers, because you're converting people who
already want to buy.

Why speed matters so much
When someone fills in your form or calls you, they have a need right now. Every hour you wait,
three things can happen: they find someone else, the urgency fades, or they simply forget they
asked. None of those show up in your reports. The lead just quietly goes cold.

How to reply faster when you're busy
1. Set up instant acknowledgements. A friendly automatic reply confirming you got their message
   and when you'll respond.
2. Get alerts where you'll see them. Send new leads to your phone as an SMS, WhatsApp or
   Telegram message, not just to an inbox you check at night.
3. Use saved replies. Write your answers to the five most common questions once and reuse
   them.

Frequently asked questions
What is a good lead response time?
An instant automatic acknowledgement, and a personal reply within the hour during business
hours. Same day is the minimum.
Do automatic replies count as responding?
They help, because they stop people moving on while they wait. They don't replace a real, personal
reply.
What should my first reply to a lead say?
Thank them by name, answer their question or confirm what they need, and suggest a clear next
step, like a time to call or a booking link.

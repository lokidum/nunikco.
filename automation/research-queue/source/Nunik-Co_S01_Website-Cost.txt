A small business website in Australia can cost anything from a monthly subscription for a DIY
builder up to tens of thousands of dollars for a large custom agency build. For most local service
businesses, a professionally built site of 5 to 15 pages sits in the low thousands, plus a small
monthly amount for hosting and updates. What changes the price is the number of pages, custom
design, features like booking or online payments, and whether SEO is included.

What drives the price up
1. Number of pages. Each service, location or product page takes writing, design and setup.
2. Custom design. A design made for your brand costs more than a template, and usually converts
   better.
3. Features. Online booking, payments, a shop, a members area or integrations with your other
   tools all add work.
4. Content. If you need someone to write the words and source photos, that's extra time.
5. SEO setup. Proper schema, page speed work, redirects from an old site and AI search setup
   take skill. Some cheap sites skip all of it.

The ongoing costs nobody mentions upfront
    Domain name: a .com.au domain is a small yearly cost
    Hosting: where your site lives, often bundled with support
    Updates: changing prices, adding services, fixing things that break
    Email: a professional address on your domain
    Paid tools: booking systems, forms or plugins with their own subscriptions
Ask any provider for the full first-year cost, not just the build price.

Is a cheap website worth it?
Sometimes. A simple, fast site with clear information beats no site at all. The risk with very cheap
builds is what's missing underneath: slow loading, no SEO setup, no mobile testing, and no one to
call when something breaks. A website that doesn't bring in enquiries isn't cheap at any price.
The better question is what a new customer is worth to you. If one new client covers the cost of the
site, and the site brings in several a month, the price matters much less than whether it actually
works. Rams Driving Academy's site brings in about 5 new paying students a month. See how.

Frequently asked questions
How much should a small business pay for a website in Australia?
For a professionally built 5 to 15 page site for a local business, expect to pay in the low thousands,
plus hosting. Simple one-page sites cost less; online shops and custom features cost more.
Is Wix or Squarespace good enough for a small business?
They can be, especially when you're starting out and have time to build it yourself. You'll have less
control over speed and SEO than with a custom build, and you'll keep paying the subscription.

      What Is an AI Receptionist, and Is It
      Worth It for a Tradie?
      What an AI receptionist does, how it handles calls you miss on the job, what it can't
      do, and how to tell if it's worth it.
      By Lokesh Barani Kumar, founder of Nunik Co. · November 2026



An AI receptionist is a voice assistant that answers your business phone when you can't. It greets
callers in a natural voice, finds out what they need, takes their details, answers common questions,
books them in or sends you the message, and works around the clock. For tradies and other
owners who miss calls while working, it can be the difference between winning the job and the
caller ringing the next business on Google.

What an AI receptionist actually does
    Answers calls you miss, after hours or while you're busy
    Asks the questions you'd ask: name, suburb, what the job is, how urgent
    Answers common questions using your information: service areas, hours, rough pricing
    Books appointments into your calendar, or flags urgent calls for a call back
    Sends you a summary by SMS, email or WhatsApp straight after the call

What it can't (and shouldn't) do
    Give firm quotes on jobs that need someone to look at them
    Handle a complicated complaint the way you would
    Replace the relationship you build when you call back personally
Think of it as a very good first point of contact. Its job is to make sure nobody hangs up without
leaving their details.

Should callers know it's an AI?
We think yes. A short line upfront, like "you're speaking with the AI assistant for [business]", is
honest and doesn't put people off when the assistant is genuinely helpful. Pretending to be a
person tends to backfire when callers work it out.

Frequently asked questions
Do AI receptionists sound robotic?
The current generation sounds far more natural than old phone menus. Most callers can have a
normal conversation with one.
Can an AI receptionist book appointments?
Yes, if it's connected to your calendar or booking system. It can also send you the details to
confirm yourself.
Is an AI receptionist better than an answering service?
It's available around the clock and can connect directly to your systems. A human answering
service can handle more complicated calls. Some businesses use both.

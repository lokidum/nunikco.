## Review request (send to every past client, one by one)

Send it the same way you normally talk to each client (text, WhatsApp or email). Ask everyone, not just the clients you expect a good review from. Asking only happy clients is review gating, which Google and the ACCC treat as misleading. Don't offer anything in return, and don't write the review for them.

> Hi [name], thanks again for working with us on [project]. If you have two minutes, a Google review would help other small businesses find us. Here's the link: [your GBP review link: Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews"]. Honest is best, whatever you thought. Cheers, Loki

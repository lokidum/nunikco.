For a local small business in Australia, a professionally built site of 5 to 15 pages usually costs in the low thousands, plus a monthly amount for hosting and support. A DIY builder costs less upfront but more of your time. A large custom agency build can run into the tens of thousands.

I run a small web studio in Canberra, so I'm not neutral, but these are the prices we publish:

- Small local business site: from $1,250
- 6 to 15 page business site: $2,400
- SEO setup bundled with a build: $600
- Hosting and support: $45 a month

Whoever you talk to, these five things move the price:

1. The number of pages.
2. Custom design or a template.
3. Features like booking, payments or a shop.
4. Who writes the words.
5. Whether real SEO setup is included (schema, speed work, redirects from an old site).

Two questions to ask any provider:

1. What is the full first-year cost, including domain, hosting, email and any paid booking or form tools?
2. Do I own the site and the domain once it's paid for, and is there a lock-in contract?

If they won't answer either clearly, keep looking.

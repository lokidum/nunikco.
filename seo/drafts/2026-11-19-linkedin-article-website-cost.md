**Title:** How much does a small business website cost in Australia? Here are our real prices.

Most web designers won't tell you their price until you've been on a call. We publish ours.

I run Nunik Co., a small web studio in Canberra, so read this knowing I sell websites. Here is what a small business site costs, and what to ask whoever you hire.

**What we charge**

- Small local business site: from $1,250
- Business website, 6 to 15 pages: $2,400
- SEO, AEO and GEO foundation, bundled with a build: $600
- Logo design: $650
- Hosting and support: $45 a month

We're not registered for GST, so there's nothing on top. You own the site and the domain once it's paid for, and there's no lock-in.

**What pushes the price up**

1. **More pages.** Every service or suburb page needs writing and setup.
2. **Custom design.** It costs more than a template and usually converts better.
3. **Features.** Booking, payments, a shop or links to your other tools.
4. **Content.** Someone has to write the words and find the photos.
5. **SEO setup.** Schema, speed work and redirects from an old site. Cheap builds often skip this.

**The costs nobody mentions upfront**

Domain, hosting, updates, email on your domain, and any paid booking or form tools. Ask every provider for the full first-year cost, not just the build price.

**Is a cheap website worth it?**

Sometimes. A simple, fast site beats no site at all. The better question is what one new customer is worth to you. One driving school site we built brings in about 5 new paying students a month. At that rate, the price of the build matters far less than whether the site actually works.
